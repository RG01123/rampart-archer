Rampart Archer

You hold the wall alone. They come from the dark edge of the field.

Touch anywhere and slide: up raises the bow for distance, down lowers it for a flat, close shot. Sideways to aim. Release to loose. Aim for the head.

Tap the flame button to swap to fire bolts. You get a few each wave. Fire spreads between enemies that touch.

Shoot the glowing things that dart across the field. Orange gives fire bolts, pale blue gives silver bolts that kill in one hit, gold gives a three-bolt volley.

Barrels roll through when the pack is close. A fire bolt blows one up, and anything near it. Roll your own from the wall with the barrel button, two per wave.

The target range gives you 20 bolts and a mix of still and moving targets. Rings score 10, 7, 5 and 3; moving targets count double.
